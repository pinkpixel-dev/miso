title: Keyboard shortcuts
description: What the keyboard does in Miso, and the one shortcut that fires while you are typing.
sidebar:
  order: 14
---

Press `?` anywhere for the list.

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| `?` | Show the shortcut list |
| `Space` | Play and pause |
| `Ctrl` or `Cmd` with `Enter` | Generate, without leaving the prompt box |
| `F` | Flip between takes on the [Compare screen](/guides/compare/) |

Nothing fires while you are typing, apart from generate, which is meant to.

That exception is the whole point of it. You write a prompt, press Ctrl and Enter, and the job queues without your hands leaving the keyboard.
